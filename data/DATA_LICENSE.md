# Data Licence and Confidentiality Notice

## Synthetic instance

The file `synthetic_instance.json` in this directory is a **synthetic dataset** created solely for reproducibility and demonstration of the model implementation. All numerical values (port demand, initial stock, silo capacity, vessel deadweight, coordinates, and cost parameters) are fictional and do not correspond to any real operation, company, or industrial partner. The synthetic instance is released under the same MIT licence as the source code.

## Real industrial data

The real industrial data used in the computational experiments reported in the associated paper is confidential and is subject to a non-disclosure agreement with the industrial partner. Real data is **not** included in this repository and cannot be shared publicly.

Bona fide researchers who wish to attempt reproduction of the published results on the real instance may contact the corresponding author. Access is granted at the discretion of the authors and the industrial partner, and requires a signed confidentiality agreement.
